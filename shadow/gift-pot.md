# PENDING Gift Pot

## Purpose
Let users turn recovered money into planned gifts, then optionally supplement it with their own money.

Example:
Christmas Pot target: £500
- £175 allocated from genuine PENDING recoveries
- £125 user top-ups
- £200 remaining

## Funding methods
1. Allocate part/all of a recovery
2. Manual user top-up
3. Scheduled/regular top-up plan
4. Future: regulated/open-banking/payment-provider integration where appropriate

## Regular top-up concept
User chooses an amount and cadence, e.g. £20 monthly.
Until a compliant payment/banking integration exists, PENDING records/reminds the plan only; it must not imply that money has moved or is held by PENDING.

A future implementation could link users to create a standing order or use an authorised payment provider. Do not collect bank credentials directly.

## Gift completion
When target/date approaches:
- compare gift-card value
- direct retailer deal
- cashback route
- PENDING partner incentive
- voucher/loyalty route
- allow user to choose cash/non-card gifting instead

## Accounting
Keep separate:
- recovered money
- user's own top-ups
- promotional/bonus value
- cashback pending
- cashback confirmed

## Potential business revenue
Gift-card/retailer commissions, affiliate revenue, partner-funded bonus value, and clearly labelled sponsored placements. Commercial payment cannot silently override best-value comparison.
