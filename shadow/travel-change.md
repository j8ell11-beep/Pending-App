# PENDING Travel Change

## Proposition
EVERY COIN. EVERY NOTE. HAS A PLACE.

Stop leftover holiday cash disappearing into drawers.

## Travel Drawer
Record physical foreign cash at home by:
- currency
- amount
- coins / notes / mixed
- optional trip/source
- approximate GBP value using timestamped FX data

## Four routes
### CONVERT
Where an available provider accepts the denomination/currency, compare gross value, fees and net GBP.

### KEEP
If conversion is poor or the user expects another trip, assign it to a Next Holiday Pot. The cash remains physically with the user; PENDING remembers where and how much.

### GIFT
Mark some/all currency for a friend/family member travelling to that destination. PENDING records the handover; it does not claim a financial transfer occurred.

### DONATE
Where a legitimate charity/currency-donation route accepts the currency, provide that route and record the user's chosen outcome.

## Decision helper
PENDING may say conversion appears uneconomic only when supported by current rates, provider eligibility and fees. Never assume all foreign coins can be exchanged.

## Future partners
- currency processors / travel-money providers
- airports / airlines / travel companies
- cash and coin kiosk networks
- charities accepting foreign currency
- regulated payment/e-money partners

## Accounting
Foreign cash remains separate from:
- money recovered
- GBP Cash Drop
- digital round-ups
- user top-ups
- promotional value

Only count converted money toward a GBP Pot after the conversion/deposit is confirmed.
