# PENDING Work

A higher-value version for sole traders, freelancers and small businesses.

Track:
- invoices awaiting payment
- customer deposits
- supplier refunds
- expenses/reimbursements
- quotes awaiting response
- warranties/returns
- insurance/service claims
- promised callbacks/actions

Potential paid capabilities:
- business dashboard
- ageing buckets
- structured chase history
- invoice/payment reminders
- evidence attachments
- team/delegation
- exports
- accounting integrations later

This remains distinct from consumer PENDING so the core consumer experience stays simple.
