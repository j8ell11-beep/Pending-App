# PENDING — £100 Challenge shadow campaign

Status: NOT PUBLIC / NOT WIRED INTO V3.2.

## Funnel
Short-form content / referral
→ "Can PENDING find £100 you've forgotten about?"
→ free 10-minute Money Sweep
→ personal amount identified
→ shareable result card
→ PENDING dashboard
→ later PENDING+ offer after demonstrated value.

## Guardrails
- £100 is a challenge target, never a guaranteed recovery claim.
- Distinguish money identified from money actually recovered.
- Never publish aggregate or average user figures without real consented data.
- No fabricated testimonials or recovery statistics.
- Sharing must be user initiated.
- Avoid exposing companies, references, names or individual item details on result cards by default.

## Future result card
Show only:
- amount identified
- number of loose ends
- sweep duration (optional)
- whether challenge target was reached
- PENDING branding
- "How much money have you forgotten about?"

## Future aggregate concept
"The Great British PENDING Pot" stays disabled until there is a secure backend, explicit analytics/consent design, and enough genuine data to make the aggregate meaningful.
