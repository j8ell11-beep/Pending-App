# PENDING Benefit — employer-funded access

## Proposition
Employers fund access to PENDING as an employee financial/life-admin wellbeing benefit.

Employee:
- gets private PENDING account
- Money Sweep / recovery tools
- reminders, goals and recovery allocation
- employer cannot see individual items, amounts, counterparties or case history

Employer:
- purchases seats/licences
- manages eligibility only
- sees billing/seat usage
- may receive only sufficiently aggregated, privacy-safe adoption metrics where appropriate

## Revenue options to test later
- per eligible employee per month/year
- active-seat pricing
- enterprise annual contract
- co-funded PENDING+ upgrade

No price is fixed yet; validate willingness-to-pay before publishing B2B pricing.

## Privacy boundary
Employment sponsorship must never grant the employer access to an employee's private PENDING content.
