# PENDING Cash Drop Network

## Idea
Turn physical loose change into purposeful money without PENDING needing to manufacture coin-counting machines.

Example:
FOUND £2.57 DOWN THE SOFA
→ take coins to supported Cash Drop point
→ pour/hand over coins
→ value counted
→ identify PENDING destination
→ provider shows any fee
→ regulated partner credits the linked destination/account
→ PENDING records the contribution to the chosen pot

## Physical routes
### 1. Coin-counting kiosk
Integrate with existing kiosk operators/hardware rather than owning machines initially.
Potential form: kiosk counts mixed coins → user scans PENDING QR / selects supported destination → transfer instruction.

### 2. Retail cash network
Small-value cash route through a participating retail cash-deposit network where supported.

### 3. Bank/e-money destination
Actual funds must settle with an appropriately regulated banking/payment/e-money partner. PENDING remains the purpose/allocation/tracking layer unless its regulatory model changes.

## Fee strategy
PENDING should compare gross coins, provider fee, partner-funded subsidy/bonus and NET amount reaching the user's destination.
Commercial goal: negotiate a low-fee or subsidised PENDING route so users are not discouraged from depositing genuinely small amounts.

## Potential commercial arrangements
- transaction revenue share
- retailer/kiosk partner fee
- bank/e-money integration contract
- partner-funded fee subsidy
- gift-card conversion commission
- charity-giving route where appropriate

## Integrity
Never label £2.57 as deposited if only £2.20 reaches the destination. Show gross, fee and net separately.
