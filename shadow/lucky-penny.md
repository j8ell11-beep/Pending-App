# PENDING Lucky Penny

## Purpose
Turn even the smallest piece of found money into a deliberate action rather than letting it disappear back into everyday spending or a drawer.

### Core language
**NO PENNY FORGOTTEN.**

Supporting platform line:
**Every coin. Every note. Has a place.**

The familiar "find a penny" idea is inspiration for the behaviour, but PENDING should use its own original campaign wording rather than depend on reproducing the traditional rhyme.

## Interaction
When a user records found money:

LUCKY PENNY
You found £0.01 / £2.57 / another amount.
What happens next?

- SAVE IT — put it towards a goal
- GROW IT — add it to PENDING Change
- GIFT IT — pass the value/physical money to someone
- GIVE IT — direct it to a supported charity route

## Lucky Penny ledger
Track separately:
- found
- put towards goals
- gifted
- given

Found money is not "recovered money".

## Sharing / discovery
A future user-initiated Lucky Penny gift could carry a simple invitation such as:
**Passing a little luck on to you.**

The recipient may discover PENDING through the gift. Do not auto-message contacts or expose financial details.

## Connections
Lucky Penny can route into:
- PENDING Cash Drop
- PENDING Change
- Gift Pots
- Goals
- Travel Change when the found currency is foreign
- charity routes

## Live-money safeguard
Current implementation is a planning/ledger concept only. PENDING must not say a gift, donation or deposit occurred until a real provider confirms the transaction.
